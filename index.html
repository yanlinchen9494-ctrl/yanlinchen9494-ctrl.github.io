01 / ABOUT

Robotic mechanisms, models and control.

I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from South China University of Technology and am now a postdoctoral researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and the Medical Robotics Innovation Technology Centre.

My work covers cable-driven and parallel mechanisms, reconfigurable mobile robots, wearable assistance and soft manipulation. I study how geometry, stiffness, friction and contact affect robot performance, and develop models and control methods that I evaluate on physical prototypes.

Recent research interests include task-adaptive robot bodies, human–robot collaboration, multimodal perception and flexible assembly. My experience spans mechanical design, fabrication, electronics, embedded control, simulation and experimental validation.

02 / RESEARCH

Research areas

01

Cable-driven & parallel mechanisms

Anthropomorphic manipulators and compact parallel mechanisms. Kinematic optimization, workspace analysis, geometric error modeling and stiffness regulation for precise, compliant motion.

02

Reconfigurable mobile robots

Wheel-leg and propeller-assisted robots that adapt their configuration for locomotion and manipulation. Body reorientation, contact stability and whole-body control in constrained environments.

03

Wearable robots & human augmentation

Lightweight wearable arms and relocatable bases for task-dependent reach. Human–robot coordination, active perception and shared-workspace planning for cooperative and parallel tasks.

04

Robot dynamics & adaptive control

Rigid–flexible dynamics, cable and joint friction, physically constrained parameter identification and sensorless force estimation. Adaptive feedforward compensation and compliant interaction control.

05

Soft robots & autonomous manipulation

Origami actuators, multimodal soft hands and deformable-object manipulation. Visual and tactile feedback, object-state estimation and motion prediction for grasping, catching and flexible assembly.

06

Precision & medical robotics

Compact mechanisms and integrated tools for confined-space operation. Hybrid positioning platforms, visual–force-guided microassembly and compliant tissue interaction.
